St. Edward Eagles Sports Weekly

Issue No. 1 · Week of November 7–13, 2026 · Lakewood, Ohio · All times Eastern

1. Top story: Eagles win the Holy War, 38–19, and move on

Final — OHSAA Division I, Region 1 quarterfinal (Fri, Nov 6): St. Edward 38, St. Ignatius 19
Quarter by quarter: St. Edward 3–14–7–14; St. Ignatius 7–0–0–12
  • Slow start, fast answer: St. Ignatius struck first after recovering an Eagle fumble, scoring one play later for a 7–0 lead. St. Edward answered with 17 straight points — a 34-yard Dallas Nixon field goal, a Langston Lucas touchdown run, and a 61-yard Lucas strike to Maxwell Jemison — and never trailed again.
  • Lucas does it both ways: The senior quarterback threw for 195 yards and a touchdown and ran for 125 yards and two scores (MaxPreps game recap).
  • Defense slams the door: Miami (Ohio) commit Landon Lehmer intercepted two different Wildcat quarterbacks, both picks killing promising drives — one at the goal line just before halftime (Kee On Sports). Lehmer leads Ohio Division I with 6 interceptions on the season (MaxPreps).
  • Supporting cast: Joe Moore caught 6 passes for 104 yards, and Ronnie Neal added a touchdown run as the Eagles piled up points in a wild 26-point fourth quarter.
  • The win makes it 10–1 on the season and sends the No. 1 seed into the regional semifinals. It is St. Edward’s 32nd playoff appearance; the program owns seven state titles (2010, 2014, 2015, 2018, 2021, 2022, 2023).

2. Tonight: Regional semifinal — Hudson at St. Edward

  • Kickoff: Friday, November 13, 7:00 PM at First Federal Lakewood Stadium. No. 1 seed St. Edward (10–1) hosts No. 5 seed Hudson (9–3), which beat Jackson 44–14 last week. The winner gets the Mentor / Canton McKinley winner in the regional final on Nov 20.
  • Watch / listen: Stream on OHSAA.tv ($9.99, an OHSAA event); free audio stream via the St. Edward Athletics site. Tickets through the OHSAA website — school season passes are not valid for playoff games.
  • Key matchup: Hudson leans on QB Hayden Lipinski (1,002 passing yards, 15 TDs; also a running threat) and RB Reno Ferri (1,495 rushing yards, 22 TDs). The Eagles counter with Lucas (1,415 passing yards, 16 TDs, plus a team-high 777 rushing yards and 7 TDs entering tonight), TE Joe Moore (36 catches, 607 yards, 8 TDs) and RB Richard Neal (499 yards, 4 TDs) — per the Akron Beacon Journal’s Week 13 breakdown.
  • Ranking: St. Edward sits No. 2 in Ohio in the On3/Massey Top 25 (Nov 8). The Beacon Journal staff pick: St. Edward 28, Hudson 14.
  • Gameday graphic: @SEHS_FOOTBALL official gameday post (Instagram).

3. Cross country: Russo closes the season at the state meet

  • Phoenix Russo finished 48th in the Division I boys race at the OHSAA State Championships at Fortress Obetz on Saturday, Nov 7, running 16:10.1 — a 20-second improvement on his regional time (11th, 16:30.9 at Boardman, where the Eagles placed 7th as a team).
  • Russo raced as an individual qualifier; the D1 boys team title chase was won by Springboro (107 points). Photo from the state meet: @sehs_xctf (Instagram).

4. Winter sports are underway

  • Hockey: The season opens this weekend. Varsity Green hosts Harborcreek tonight (Fri Nov 13, 6:00 PM, Winterhurst Ice Arena), then plays at Wooster (Sat, 1:10 PM) and at Kent (Sun, 4:25 PM). Varsity Gold is at the Governor’s Cup showcase in South Bend: Providence Catholic on Saturday (12:00 PM, Compton Family Ice Arena at Notre Dame) and North Allegheny on Sunday. The preview from the Greater Ohio Hockey League notes 11 returning players from a 21–15 team that reached the Brooklyn Regional Final; players to watch: Sean Gilliam, Andrew Gray, Leo DeAngelo and Owen Scanlon. St. Edward has won 11 state hockey championships.
  • Basketball: Scrimmage season starts Saturday — varsity and JV host Canton GlenOak (10:00 AM), and the freshman squads host Elyria Catholic (12:00 PM). More scrimmages: at Garfield Heights (Thu Nov 19) and home vs. North Royalton (Sat Nov 21). The regular season tips off with a Foundation Game at Gilmour on Nov 27 and the opener at Lakewood on Dec 1.
  • Bowling: The bowling Eagles open Saturday morning (Nov 14, 8:00 AM) at AMF Brookgate Lanes — varsity vs. St. Ignatius, JV Green vs. Padua, JV Gold vs. St. Ignatius.
  • On the horizon: Wrestling and swimming seasons open later this month — watch this space.
