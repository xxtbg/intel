Post-Midterm Market Report

Tuesday, November 10, 2026. Prices: Hyperliquid, ~5:30 AM ET.

1. Lame-duck Iran escalation risk

  • Strike pause, blockade stays: Trump said Monday he will hold off on fresh strikes on Iran for now, after appeals from Gulf states including Qatar's prime minister in White House talks, but will keep the US naval blockade of Hormuz in place. He called the conflict militarily "effectively over" while keeping the posture. (Reuters via TBS News, Nov 10; AnewZ, Nov 10)
  • Fighting at sea has not paused: Iranian state media say the US struck Qeshm Island late Sunday; tanker attacks in the Gulf/Hormuz are at their highest rate since the war began, with 11 tankers "disabled" in 10 days per Iranian state TV. Shipping collapsed over the weekend: 5 commodity vessels transited Saturday, none Sunday, vs 31 the prior weekend. Iran's ask remains blockade lifted + some sanctions relief in exchange for reopening the strait. (Reuters via TBS News, Nov 10; B2B Market Signal, Nov 10)
  • Oil unwind accelerates: Brent settled -5.85% Monday at $92.57 and fell further Tuesday to ~$91.88 (futures), now ~22% below the Oct 30 high of $118. Hyperliquid is lower still: BRENTOIL $89.06 (24h -7.4%, 7d -16.5%, volume $308M, OI ~3.34M units), CL $84.26 (24h -5.9%, 7d -14.5%). The market is pricing de-escalation even though no deal exists and the blockade — the core obstacle — remains.
  • The window: the Republican-controlled 119th Congress sits until the 120th seats on January 3, 2027. Nearer tests: federal funding runs out December 11 (war funding fight), and any strike resumption would reprice oil fast given how much premium has already left.

2. DEX and privacy-coin inflows — regulatory and sanctions risk

  • MEXC ban — clock still not started: FinCEN's Nov 6 order under Operation Economic Outcast (Section 9714) bars US institutions from fund transmittals to/from MEXC Global over at least $16B in illicit flows (May 2021–May 2026), incl. ~$1.5B Russian sanctions evasion and ~$1B with 40+ Iranian exchanges; OFAC also designated 8 Canadian MSBs tied to Iranian networks. The order takes effect 30 days after Federal Register publication — as of latest reporting it is still a PDF pending publication, so no effective date yet. MEXC says operating normally; OKX has warned users against direct transfers from MEXC. (CoinDesk, Nov 7; Crypto.news, Nov 8; Bitcoin Magazine, Nov 6)
  • Market structure — no new text: CLARITY remains stalled after the Senate's 49–50 cloture failure on Sep 15; it dies Jan 3 if not passed in the lame duck. House Democrats' own bill — built on Waters' investor-protection amendment, stricter DeFi rules, more SEC / less CFTC, ethics curbs on officials — is still drafting with no text or introduction. (Unchained, Nov 5; Decrypt, Nov 5; Rebatly update, Nov 10)
  • Privacy coins break down: XMR $543.08 (24h -8.6%, 7d -13.4%, volume $30M) and ZEC $1,141.50 (24h -5.5%, 7d -3.4%, volume $343M) led the fall; spot trackers show the privacy sector -3.6% weighted on the day. The sanctions-risk bid is not offsetting broader risk-off selling — the inflow thesis is weakening, not confirming.
  • DEX tape mixed: HYPE $73.93 (24h +1.3%, 7d -12.6%, volume $406M — heaviest here) stabilized after last week's slide; Hyperliquid perps did ~$10.9B in 24h across 349 markets. ARB $0.1661 (24h -6.6%, 7d +3.9%) gave back yesterday's buck-the-tape gain. No DEX inflow confirmation yet.

3. Musk companies — political risk premium

  • Bounce, not reversal: Hyperliquid perps: TSLA $376.18 (24h +4.6%, 7d -6.5%), SPCX $142.11 (24h +4.5%, 7d +3.7% — back above its $135 June IPO price). That follows ~$450–456B in combined market value lost in the two sessions after the midterms (TSLA $398.50 → $343.69 cash close Nov 5; SPCX $141.31 → $123.62). One up day after that drawdown does not clear the premium. *
  • Why the premium persists: Democratic control of both chambers, a flipped Texas (governor and a Senate seat), expected subpoenas/investigations into Musk, federal contracts (~$6.78B booked for SpaceX by early October, incl. Golden Dome), data-center scrutiny (Goldman flagged possible state moratoria touching ~45% of planned capacity), and tougher odds for a Tesla–SpaceX merger. (Newszest, Nov 6; Summa Money / Motley Fool, Nov 6; HeyGoTrade, Nov 6)
  • Timing: the new Congress is not seated until Jan 3, 2027, and investigations take time — the risk is headline/oversight overhang through the lame duck, not immediate legislation (any bill still faces a presidential veto).

4. Rotation and macro

  • Defensive rotation continues: Goldman Sachs' Lou Miller says prime-book beta-adjusted net exposure is at very low levels vs history, data-center stocks are -36% from highs (NeoCloud -57%), and healthcare is pitched as the next defensive/AI leg with accelerating earnings. Pre-midterm, 9 of 11 S&P sectors rose Oct 5–Nov 3 while tech fell 12.2%; post-vote analyst picks add clean energy, drones and hospitals (HCA +4.6%, UHS +11.1% in the vote week). Hyperliquid SP500 $7,555.60 (24h +1.9%, 7d +0.3%) — index bouncing, leadership still defensive. (Goldman via Medical Buyer, Nov 9–10; BloFin, Nov 5; NashNova, Nov 7)
  • Fed is the near-term driver: October CPI lands today (Tue Nov 10, 8:30 AM ET) — the last inflation print before the Dec 8–9 FOMC. After the 165k payrolls beat (vs ~65k expected, unemployment 4.1%), futures price ~78–85% odds of a 25bp December hike to 4.00–4.25%; Governor Waller said Tuesday he will back at least 25bp even if CPI cools, and would consider 50bp if core runs ≥0.3% m/m. A hot print hits the growth/tech side of the rotation hardest. (Reuters via Hindu BusinessLine, Nov 10; Economics Desk, Nov 7)
