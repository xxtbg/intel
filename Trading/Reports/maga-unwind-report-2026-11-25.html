1. Lame-duck Iran escalation risk

  • War funded through January — lame-duck window is the funded window: Trump told the New York Post the Iran war is funded "until really the end of January" and warned Democrats that restricting the campaign could turn a US victory into defeat. That funding runway covers the whole lame-duck period before the new Democratic Congress is seated Jan 3, 2027. (The AZB / NY Post interview, Nov 24-25)
  • Leadership strikes reportedly on the table: NBC and Fox reported the administration is weighing strikes on senior Iranian leaders, including Mojtaba Khamenei; Israel's defense minister cited Iranian chemical-weapons development and left a preemptive strike open. No strike has been announced. (One Turn Iran roundup, Nov 25)
  • Hormuz mine claims escalate, still unverified: The IRGC Navy said two tankers hit naval mines and caught fire early Wednesday, a container ship stopped Tuesday after a mine strike, and Iran claimed two US drones downed over the strait. A maritime information center called an earlier mine claim unconfirmed; three vessels were struck by unidentified projectiles Monday per UKMTO. (The AZB, Nov 25; Reuters, Nov 25)
  • Oil is fading the risk, for now: Brent fell ~1.6% to $89.93 and WTI ~1.9% to $80.53 Wednesday as surplus prospects and recovering Gulf exports outweighed Hormuz attacks; OPEC+ meets Nov 29 on 2027 output policy. Hyperliquid marks tracked lower: BRENTOIL $88.29, CL $81.61. (Reuters via Euronext, Nov 25)

2. DEX / privacy inflows on regulatory & sanctions risk

  • MEXC cutoff spreads ahead of Dec 10: Japan's bitbank stops MEXC withdrawals Nov 24 and deposits Dec 10 — the day FinCEN's Operation Economic Outcast order takes effect (published in the Federal Register Nov 10). FinCEN put MEXC flows tied to sanctions evasion/cybercrime at at least $16.7B (May 2021–May 2026), incl. Iran, Russia and North Korea. Kaizen Finance also ended its MEXC cooperation and KZEN listing, pointing users to Gate.io and PancakeSwap (DEX). (CryptoNews / bitbank notice, Nov 20; Kaizen Finance, Nov 2026)
  • Privacy sector volume surged, prices did not follow: Privacy-sector 24h volume was ~$1.56B (+95%) on Nov 24 data, with ZEC volume +126% — but ZEC is -22.7% over 7 days on Hyperliquid and XMR only +0.2%. Flow is chasing liquidity/exits, not marking a clean privacy bid yet. (CoinPaprika privacy sector, Nov 24)
  • CLARITY stays dead; the Democratic rewrite is the 2027 story: No new lame-duck movement. The standing outline of the House Democrats' own bill — stricter DeFi rules than the BRCA, more jurisdiction shifted from CFTC to SEC, stronger consumer protections, ethics curbs — remains the template to watch when the new Congress sits. (Unchained, Nov 5)
  • DEX proxy weak: HYPE $57.92, -8.0% 24h / -15.5% 7d on $474.9M Hyperliquid day volume — no DEX-inflow bid showing in the perp market despite the MEXC squeeze.
