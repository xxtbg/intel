Post-Midterm Market Report

Thursday, November 26, 2026 (Thanksgiving — US cash markets closed). Prices: Hyperliquid, ~5:30 AM ET. *TSLA/SPCX/SP500/GOLD figures are Hyperliquid perp marks, not cash-market prices.

1. Lame-duck Iran escalation risk

  • Tanker war escalated in the last 24 hours: CENTCOM said US forces struck three Iranian vessels — a tanker off Kharg Island, one near Jask, and an unladen ship in the Gulf of Oman — after Iranian ballistic missiles were fired at a US carrier and destroyer, which evaded them. Iran said it also targeted three tankers transiting an "unauthorised route" in Hormuz and three US-linked vessels elsewhere, and warned all Gulf shipping against US-guided routes. (Europe Says / Al Jazeera reporting, Nov 25-26)
  • Third Hormuz tanker hit Wednesday: The IRGC said three vessels struck sea mines Wednesday, Nov 25 — two oil tankers early in the day and a liquefied-gas tanker in the afternoon — continuing an almost-daily run of mine/projectile incidents since mid-November. The mine claims remain Iranian-sourced and unverified; UKMTO has described other recent hits as unidentified projectiles. (Westminster Times / Qatar Tribune, Nov 26)
  • Oil finally reacting: Brent settled +3.5% at $94.61 Wednesday after Yemen's Houthis said they attacked Saudi Arabia's largest oil refinery, and rose further in Asia Thursday to ~$94.71-94.90; WTI ~$84.03. ANZ estimates Hormuz flows at just 49% of normal. Note the gap: Hyperliquid perp marks (table below) sit below cash futures — BRENTOIL $90.25 vs ~$94.7 cash Brent. (Reuters, Nov 26)
  • Hawks pressing to resume major strikes: Israel and administration hawks are pushing Trump to renew major attacks on Iran, paused since the summer; the leadership-strike option reported Tuesday remains on the table, and the war is funded through January — covering the entire lame-duck window before the new Congress is seated Jan 3, 2027. (Finwire, Nov 26)

2. DEX / privacy inflows on regulatory & sanctions risk

  • Privacy bid back, DEX tokens bouncing hard: On Hyperliquid, XMR +3.8% (24h) and +5.1% (7d) to $527.48 on $48M volume; ZEC +2.4% on a heavy $320M — though ZEC is still -22.4% over 7 days, so this is a bounce inside a downtrend, not a reversal yet. HYPE +6.1% to $61.38 ($517M volume, still -10.4% on the week) and RUNE +10.1% on thin $0.8M volume. The sanctions-evasion bid is selective, not broad.
  • MEXC ban effective date is the next catalyst: FinCEN's Nov 6 order under Operation Economic Outcast cuts MEXC off from the US financial system 30 days after Federal Register publication — reported as Dec 10, 2026. Treasury alleges at least $16B in illicit flows (Iran/IRGC-Qods, Russia via Garantex/Grinex, North Korea). As the cutoff nears, flows pushed off a major centralised venue are the flow to watch for DEX/privacy spillover. (CoinDesk, Nov 7; Cointelegraph, Nov 10 update)
  • CLARITY Act dies with this Congress unless revived: The bill — SEC/CFTC split, CFTC licensing for spot exchanges — failed its Senate motion 49-50 on Sep 15 and has no new vote scheduled; it expires Jan 3, 2027 when the 119th Congress ends. Regulators are filling the gap themselves: the SEC has listed major tokens as digital commodities since March 2026 and the CFTC is taking comments on its own crypto rules until Dec 14, 2026. A stricter Democratic market-structure bill in the new Congress remains the theory-2 tail risk. (CoinStats, Nov 26)

3. Musk premium: Tesla-SpaceX merger race

  • Merger talks named, year-end deadline: The Financial Times reported Nov 24 that five directors lead the two special committees — Denholm, Wilson-Thompson and Hartung for Tesla (Evercore advising); Botha and Glein for SpaceX (Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs) — and that the sides are "not yet close," though a deal by year-end remains possible. The all-stock combination under discussion is ~$3.5 trillion. (Admiral Markets summary of FT, updated Nov 25)
  • Jan 3 is the political deadline: A signed deal by Dec 31 would land days before the new Democratic Congress is seated Jan 3, 2027; incoming majorities have signalled Musk's companies, Starlink decisions and SpaceX military contracts are early investigation/subpoena targets, and Texas' incoming AG said a merger this size "warrants tremendous scrutiny." Congress cannot block the deal — antitrust sits with the DOJ/FTC — but committees can subpoena the deal record. (Electrek, Nov 24)
  • Price action: SPCX +5.4% (24h) to $128.84 on $242M — still -12.3% over 7 days; TSLA +2.3% to $382.18, -2.2% on the week (Hyperliquid perp marks; US cash market closed for Thanksgiving). The premium is event-driven and volatile, not collapsing. Next checkpoint: Tesla's annual meeting Dec 3, 2026 (3 PM CT, Gigafactory Texas) — the merger is not on the ballot, but Denholm, a lead negotiator, is up for re-election.

4. Rotation & macro

  • Hot PCE locks in a December hike: October PCE came in Wednesday at +3.8% y/y (in line) with core at +3.3% — above the 3.2% expected, highest since Nov 2023 — and Q3 GDP revised up to 4.3%. Markets price ~95-98% odds of a 25bp hike at the Dec 8-9 FOMC (to 4.00-4.25%), with a further hike possible in January/March. Next data: November jobs report Dec 4; next PCE Dec 23, after the decision. (TalkMarkets, Nov 25)
  • Defensive rotation intact: Health Care, Utilities and Consumer Staples lead this quarter; XLV hit its best level vs SPY since April 2025, and on Nov 25 healthcare +2.95% and consumer defensive +1.66% led while technology fell -3.26% and the Nasdaq 100 was the softest major index. Financials/real estate are the model's next rotation candidates but unconfirmed. (StockCharts, Nov 25; FinancialAIguru, Nov 25)
  • Equities whipsawing into the holiday: The S&P 500 snapped back +4.7% Monday after an ~8.6% down week, then gave back ground Wednesday on the hot PCE, new tariff proposals (up to 20% on 14 partners, nothing effective before late-January hearings) and a chip selloff; spot gold broke down toward ~$3,446-3,483 Wednesday even as Hyperliquid GOLD holds $3,565. US markets are closed today for Thanksgiving, with a shortened Friday session. (Topstep Weekly Market Kickoff, Nov 26)
