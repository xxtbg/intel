Post-Midterm Market Report

Saturday, November 28, 2026. Prices: Hyperliquid, ~5:30 AM ET. US cash markets closed today (Saturday). *TSLA/SPCX/SP500/GOLD figures are Hyperliquid perp marks, not cash-market prices.

1. Lame-duck Iran escalation risk

  • US disables a blockade-runner: CENTCOM said a US aircraft fired precision munitions into the stern of the Palau-flagged bulk carrier M/V Pacific Archer on Thursday (Nov 26) in the Gulf of Oman, after it left Bandar Abbas and ignored repeated warnings; propulsion was knocked out, crew unharmed. CENTCOM's tally: 158 merchant ships redirected, six disabled. Trump, at Mar-a-Lago, called it "the most successful blockade in naval history" and said a resumed strike campaign would "absolutely hit Pickaxe Mountain," the underground complex near Natanz. (New York Post, Nov 27; The Levant Files, Nov 28)
  • Tehran answers with wider threats: IRGC Aerospace Force commander Majid Mousavi warned Friday that Iran's enemies should expect military action "from the air and sea" and vowed to break the blockade. Navy commander Adm. Shahram Irani said the Supreme Leader has lifted geographic restrictions on Iranian operations. Adviser Mohammad Mokhber: "Neither will the Strait of Hormuz be reopened, nor will we allow the blockade to remain in place." (Breitbart, Nov 27; Newsmax, Nov 27)
  • Mediation still alive, terms unchanged: Qatar and Pakistan continue relaying proposals between Tehran and Washington, per Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi; Tehran wants the blockade lifted and the war ended before normal Hormuz shipping resumes. Trump has set a new-year deadline for a deal and says he is inclined to resume strikes after it if none is reached — i.e. the escalation window runs right up against the Jan 3 seating of the new Congress. (Newsmax / Jerusalem Post, Nov 27)
  • Oil: OPEC+ is tomorrow's catalyst: Cash Brent settled ~$96.57 Thursday (three-week high) after the Jazan refinery strike and Hormuz mine claims, but Hyperliquid perps softened overnight — BRENTOIL $90.56 (-1.9% 24h), CL $83.24 (-2.9%). All 21 OPEC+ members meet Sunday Nov 29 to set 2027 quotas, with ~2M bpd of cuts still in place, a UAE exit and capacity disputes in play; two sources told Reuters a rollover of current targets is likely. A quota fight is the bear case for Brent (one scenario set puts year-end at $84); a Hormuz shock that breaks the talks is the bull case (~$103). (Traders Union, Nov 26; Vektora, Nov 26)

2. DEX / privacy-coin inflows on regulatory & sanctions risk

  • MEXC cutoff now has a date: Dec 10. FinCEN's Nov 6 order under Operation Economic Outcast — citing at least $16B (order text: $16.7B) in illicit flows tied to Iranian, North Korean and Russian sanctions evasion, including $1B+ with 40+ Iranian exchangers — takes effect Dec 10, 2026, 30 days after Federal Register publication, with no closing date named. US institutions will be barred from transmitting funds to or from MEXC, crypto included. That is the near-term dated catalyst for flows looking for non-US, non-custodial venues. (FinCEN, Nov 6; PTYcoin, Nov 11)
  • CLARITY Act stays dead in the lame duck: The market-structure bill passed the House in July 2025 but Senate cloture failed 49-50 on Sep 15, 2026; no new vote is scheduled and the bill expires when this Congress ends Jan 3, 2027. Regulators are instead writing pieces of it as agency rules — SEC custody comments due Dec 7, CFTC advance notice Dec 14. No legislative DeFi framework this Congress means the stricter House-Democrat alternative and the SEC-vs-CFTC fight both roll into the new Congress. (DegenKit, Nov 20-22; CoinPerps, Nov 24)
  • DEX tape: volume holds, HYPE token soft: Hyperliquid remains the dominant perp DEX (~$212B 30-day perp volume, ~33% of perp-DEX volume in the latest DefiLlama reading; $12.2B 24h perp volume and $5.5B open interest on Nov 20). But the regulatory-warning stack (Hong Kong SFC alert list Nov 18, plus FCA/MAS warnings) and Abraxas Capital's full exit — ~$13.4B cumulative volume, ~$530M profit — hit the token mid-week: HYPE fell ~7% on Nov 25. On Hyperliquid today HYPE is $62.89 (-4.4% 24h) on a heavy $411M day volume, still +5.9% over 7 days. (Datawallet, Nov 20 data; CryptoTimes, Nov 25)
  • Privacy coins leaking, not bid: No sanctions-flow bid is showing in price yet: XMR $437.80 (-2.3% 24h, -7.8% 7d) and ZEC $731.13 (-3.4% 24h, -4.0% 7d), though ZEC volume is heavy at $253M/day. If the Dec 10 MEXC cutoff pushes flows on-chain, these are the first places it would show; so far it hasn't.

3. Musk premium: TSLA / SPCX

  • TSLA rips into the merger question: Tesla closed the shortened Friday session at $400.00 (+5.2%) per market data, and the Hyperliquid perp is $398.85 (+2.7% 24h, +13.4% 7d) — the strongest 7-day move in today's table. The driver is the possible all-stock Tesla-SpaceX merger (~$3.5T combined value reported), not car fundamentals. (Monafy / Yahoo Finance, Nov 27)
  • Control is the story: HSBC's Michael Tyndall: "We see control as the key motivation for a merger." Analysts estimate Musk could hold ~55-58% of combined-company votes (he has ~20% of Tesla votes, ~84% of SpaceX's). A deal needs approval from a majority of ALL outstanding Tesla shares — non-votes count as no — plus regulatory review; no filing or terms exist yet, and one Barron's estimate puts any transaction 12-18 months away. SPCX perp is flat by comparison: $131.91 (-0.5% 24h, +1.6% 7d). (TheStreet, Nov 27; Webull, Nov 27)
  • Two dated tests ahead: Tesla's annual meeting Dec 3 (chair Robyn Denholm faces re-election) and the EU's FSD (Supervised) technical-committee decision, pushed from Dec 1 to Dec 15 — EU-wide approval needs 15 of 27 member states with 65% of population. The Nov 25 intraday -7% drop on merger + EU-delay headlines shows how fast the premium can move both ways. (MiTrade / TradingKey, Nov 27)

4. Post-midterm restructuring / other market movers

  • Defensive rotation is now the month's story: Since Oct 1, consumer staples +15.3%, utilities +10.1% and healthcare +9.7%, while technology is -17.6% and communication services -14.8%; the S&P 500 is -5.7% over the same span with seven of eleven sectors still positive — losses are concentrated in the big AI names, energy offered no shelter (-1.8%). November also saw the Nvidia leadership turmoil (Huang resigned, then was reinstated) and a hot CPI print that pushed the 10-year back above 5.1%; markets now price almost three Fed hikes over the next 12 months. (Ventum Financial, Nov 26)
  • Hyperliquid tape disagrees with the cash-market month, mildly: SP500 perp $7,289.8 is +4.6% over 7 days and GOLD $3,663.3 is +3.6% over 7 days — equities and the hedge rising together off the late-month lows, consistent with a bounce inside a defensive rotation rather than a return to risk-on.
