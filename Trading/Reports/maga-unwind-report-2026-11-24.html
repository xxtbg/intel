1. Lame-duck Iran escalation risk

  • Strike decision still pushed past the New Year: GlobalSecurity's Day 269 update (Nov 23, 0500 ET cutoff) confirms no US or Israeli strike on Iranian territory was acknowledged in the Nov 22-23 window, and Trump told Fox News Nov 22 he is "inclined" to resume strikes after the New Year if no deal is reached — after the new Democratic Congress is seated Jan 3, 2027. Ynet reported a US decision to delay any attack by at least a week. The lame-duck escalation window keeps narrowing. (GlobalSecurity, Nov 23)
  • Hormuz attacks continue, unconfirmed tanker strike in Gulf of Oman: Seven tankers have been hit since Nov 16 (Joint Maritime Information Centre via Gulf News); UKMTO reported a tanker incident "involving military forces" in the Gulf of Oman on Nov 22, with a maritime analyst's claim of a US strike on an Iranian-oil tanker unconfirmed by either side. Iran's claims of two supertankers hitting mines and a 20th US drone downed remain unverified. Hormuz transits ran 114 vessels Nov 19-21 with US assistance, below the 2025 average of 138/day. (GlobalSecurity, Nov 23)
  • Oil crashes through the Hormuz risk: Cash Brent settled Monday at $89.37, down $3.20 / -3.5% in one session (intraday $86.58 in some feeds, first below $89 since late August), extending the fall from $117.64 on Oct 30 to roughly -26% in under four weeks. Tuesday early trade held near $88.97. Surplus forecasts and the market's view that Hormuz is partially reopened are overwhelming live tanker attacks. Hyperliquid agrees: BRENTOIL $86.69 (-2.9% 24h), CL $80.03 (-3.6% 24h). Next catalyst: OPEC+ meets Sunday Nov 29 to set 2027 quotas, with Saudi needing ~$94-96 for its wartime budget. (Energy360 / EuropeSays, Nov 23-24; AsiaToday, Nov 24)

2. DEX / privacy inflows on regulatory & sanctions risk

  • CLARITY still dead, no new vote: As of Nov 18-20, Congress.gov still lists the Sep 15 failed 49-50 cloture (motion to reconsider) as the last action on H.R. 3633; no new Senate vote in the lame duck, and the 119th Congress expires Jan 3, 2027. Senate Agriculture heard CFTC nominees Nov 17 and Banking heard SEC nominees Nov 19, with no committee votes scheduled by Nov 20. (Banking on Bitcoin / Crypto Regulation Briefing, updated Nov 20)
  • MEXC cutoff — Dec 10, now 16 days out: FinCEN's Nov 6 Section 9714 order designating MEXC Global a primary money-laundering concern ($16B+ / $16.7B in alleged illicit flows May 2021-May 2026, incl. ~$1.5B Russia sanctions evasion via Garantex/Grinex and ~$1B with 40+ Iranian exchanges) was published in the Federal Register Nov 10 and bars US financial institutions from transfers to/from MEXC effective Dec 10, 2026, with no end date. (Treasury / FinCEN via Coin98 and Banking on Bitcoin, Nov 6-18)
  • Democratic SEC-led rewrite remains the 2027 path: House Democrats are drafting their own market-structure bill giving the SEC, not the CFTC, the lead — building on Waters' 2025 investor-protection amendment, Senate Democrat provisions from the failed CLARITY talks, and ethics rules aimed at officials' crypto holdings. No text published yet. Stricter DeFi / SEC-heavier oversight stays structurally supportive for truly decentralized venues. (Crypto In America / Eleanor Terrett via CryptoSlate, relayed Nov 7)
  • Privacy fades on the relief rally, DEX volume holds: Privacy sector market cap +0.5% in 24h to Nov 24 with $942M combined volume — ZEC $896.7M of it, XMR $141.9M (Blocfolio / CoinGecko, Nov 24). On Hyperliquid, ZEC is the weak leg at $742.01 (-6.3% 24h, heavy day volume) and XMR $495.55 (-1.7% 24h), while HYPE holds $63.08 (-1.1% 24h). XMR perp volume across venues rose +23.9% to $131.9M in the 24h to Nov 24, with Hyperliquid holding the largest XMR open interest (~$66M). Risk-on equity flow, not regulation, drove today's crypto tape. (CoinPerps, Nov 24)

3. Musk companies — political risk premium

  • Merger talks get named committees: Tesla and SpaceX have appointed five directors to guide two special committees handling merger negotiations — Tesla: Robyn Denholm, Kathleen Wilson-Thompson, Jack Hartung; SpaceX: Roelof Botha, Randy Glein — the Financial Times reported late Monday, via Reuters (Nov 23-24). Advisers: Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs for SpaceX, Evercore for Tesla, fine-tuning models for a potential all-stock deal. No formal offer; SpaceX's filing said its committee could decline to recommend a transaction. Earlier reporting put a possible announcement as soon as December. (Reuters, Nov 24; MarketSpeaker, Nov 24)
  • Premium debate sharpens: Cathie Wood (ARK, owns both) told CNBC Nov 20 TSLA holders "will get" a premium, at least 25%; HSBC raised its TSLA target to $359 from $157, seeing $507 takeover value (33% premium to the 30-day average) vs $212 standalone. Musk has said a merger needs approval from holders of at least 50% of outstanding Tesla shares and cautioned against assuming a deal. Tesla booked $626M in Megapack sales to SpaceX in the first nine months of 2026. (TipRanks, Nov 23)
  • Monday tape: TSLA ripped, SPCX lagged the AI rally: Cash Monday: TSLA +11.5% to $385.46, SPCX +4.4% to $133.35, both lifted by Nvidia's Huang reinstatement (Musk on X: "It is done" / "The mistake has been corrected"). Hyperliquid pre-market Tuesday: TSLA $382.03 (+5.6% vs prev day), SPCX $131.95 (-0.4% vs prev day) — SPCX giving back more overnight, consistent with merger-ratio uncertainty rather than a broad Musk discount. No new congressional hearing/subpoena into Musk, DOGE or federal contracts surfaced in today's search. (TradingView / Benzinga, Nov 23; Investopedia, Nov 23)

  • The Huang reversal — the day's dominant mover: Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO/president on Monday, 11 days after his resignation, after the stock had lost ~48% / ~$2.8T from its Oct 6 peak. NVDA closed +31.5% to $165.05 (+$952B market cap, best day since 2003), Nasdaq +6.5%, S&P 500 +4.7% (best day since April 2025, snapping a 7-session losing streak), Dow +1,448 / +3.0%. Trump welcomed the reinstatement and said he helped convince the board; Huang is scheduled to testify before the Senate Banking Committee Dec 3 on chip-export / China compliance. Hyperliquid NVDA perp: $157.25 (+15.7% vs prev day, still lagging the cash close), SP500 perp $7,208.70 (+1.4%). (Reuters / CNN / Investopedia, Nov 23)
  • Rotation paused, not reversed: Monday's rally was tech-led (tech +9%, comms +5%, nine of eleven S&P sectors up — Reuters), reversing last week's broadening into Energy (+40.9% YTD), Health Care (+20.9%) and Staples (+19.4%). The post-midterm rotation out of AI/data-center builders into healthcare, staples, housing and tariff-sensitive consumer names — and out of defense (-4% to -12% on the post-election session) — remains the underlying regime; one relief day on a CEO reinstatement does not settle it. (Reuters Trading Day, Nov 23; NBOFI November commentary)
  • Fed still restrictive into December: Fed held at 3.75-4.00% on Oct 28 after September's hike; October jobs came in at +165k vs ~65k expected, unemployment 4.1%. Futures pricing for a December hike has ranged 75-92% in recent updates (Dec 9 FOMC). That, plus OPEC+ Nov 29, is the macro calendar that matters this week. GOLD on Hyperliquid was flat at $3,620.80 (+0.0% 24h) through the equity melt-up — no safe-haven unwind. (NBOFI / TechFitex, Nov 18-23)
